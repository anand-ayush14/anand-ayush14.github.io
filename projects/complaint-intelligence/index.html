    Method

    Data: CFPB Consumer Complaint Database export for product "Credit card", companies American Express, JPMorgan Chase, Capital One and Citibank, Sep 2023 – Oct 2026 (the export starts mid-August 2023; partial months and complaints still in progress were dropped). Relief = closed with monetary or non-monetary relief. The CFPB no longer publishes complaint narratives in its export, so all analysis uses the structured fields. Complaint counts are not adjusted for each issuer's number of card members. → 13 SQL queries in SQLite (CTEs, CASE, window functions: SUM OVER, LAG, RANK, moving average with ROWS BETWEEN) → spike detector (z-score against the issue's previous 6 months, at least 20 complaints) → Logistic Regression (one-hot), Random Forest and Gradient Boosting (native categorical) with TimeSeriesSplit CV and a time-based hold-out → permutation importance → cumulative gains.
