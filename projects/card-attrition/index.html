    Method

    Cleaning: renamed columns, dropped two leftover model-output columns and a blank column, verified unique IDs and no missing values, and kept "Unknown" education/income/marital values as their own category → 10 SQL queries in SQLite (CASE bands, window functions, NTILE, RANK, CTE) → features: average transaction value, Q4/Q1 activity change and 12 others. Gender, education and marital status were excluded. Open-to-buy was dropped because it equals credit limit minus revolving balance → stratified 75/25 split, 5-fold CV comparing Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting → permutation importance → K-Means on 8 scaled behavioural features, with k chosen by silhouette score → campaign profit curve under stated assumptions.
