4. Key insights

5. Recommendations

  1. Early-warning outreach after the first missed payment. Customers one month late default at ~2.5× the on-time rate. Trigger a reminder plus a payment-plan offer immediately, instead of waiting for month two (~69% default).
  2. Weekly review of the "Very high" tier (top 10% by score, ~70% default rate): reduce limits or pause new credit for these accounts.
  3. Careful limit-increase policy for low-limit accounts. Customers under NT$50K default at 36%; increase limits only after six months of clean repayment.
  4. Use the minimum-payment signal. Customers who paid nothing on a positive bill default at ~40%. Flag them for collections contact within days.
  5. Monitor fairness and drift. Keep demographic attributes out of decisions, re-check performance monthly, and retrain when AUC drops.

Method

Cleaning: merged undocumented education/marriage codes, verified 0 missing values and unique IDs → 8 SQL queries in SQLite (GROUP BY, CASE bands, window functions) → features: utilisation, max months late, late count, repayment ratio → stratified 75/25 split, 5-fold CV comparison of Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting → rank-based risk tiers evaluated on the untouched test set. Dataset: Yeh & Lien (2009), UCI Machine Learning Repository.
